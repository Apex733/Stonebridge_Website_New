Plant & Machinery FAQs

Plant & Machinery finance explainedCommon questions answered

What finance is available for agricultural businesses?

Plant & Machinery businesses can access asset finance for machinery, term loans for land or buildings, seasonal working capital, and livestock finance depending on the asset finance type.

How do lenders handle seasonal asset finance income?

Specialist agricultural lenders structure repayments around harvest cycles and seasonal income. We match you with lenders who understand asset finance rather than applying standard business criteria.

Can I finance plant and machinery?

Yes. Most new and used plant and machinery including tractors, combine harvesters, and specialist equipment can be financed through hire purchase or finance lease.

Can I borrow to invest in land or business buildings?

Yes. Commercial mortgages and secured term loans are available for land purchase, barn conversions, grain stores, and other business infrastructure. We advise on the most appropriate route.

Will applying affect my credit score?

Initial eligibility conversations are designed to understand your needs before any formal lender checks. If a credit search is required later, we will explain that before it happens.

Do you work with start-ups?

Yes, some lenders can support newer businesses depending on the finance type, trading history, security, projections, and director background.

Is there a limit to how much I can borrow?

Funding amounts depend on the product, lender appetite, business profile, affordability, and available security. Stonebridge can support requirements from smaller working capital needs through to larger facilities.

Are there any sectors you do not support?

Most mainstream UK business sectors can be considered. Some lenders may have restrictions, so we check your sector and funding purpose early in the process.

Still undecided?

You can start with a simple conversation. We will help you understand what may be possible before you commit to a full application.
