EARLY CAREERS AT STONEBRIDGE

Your Future Starts
Here.

Join a fast-growing, innovative finance company where you get real responsibility from day one. We're looking for ambitious graduates and interns to shape the future of UK business finance.

View Programmes All Open Roles

30+ Graduates Hired in 2025

Why Start With Us?

We don’t do "coffee-fetching" internships. You are a core part of the team from day one.

Real Responsibility, Fast

Forget shadowing for months. At Stonebridge, our graduates manage live client portfolios, underwrite real deals, and contribute to company-wide strategy within their first 6 months. We empower you to make decisions that actually impact the business.

Structured Mentorship

You’ll be paired with a senior director who acts as your dedicated mentor. Weekly 1-on-1s ensure you are constantly developing your skill set and navigating your career path effectively.

Unlimited Growth

We promote based on merit, not tenure. Many of our current department heads started in our graduate scheme. If you prove yourself, the sky's the limit.

Vibrant Culture & Perks

Regular team socials, a comprehensive wellness package, gym memberships, and a flexible hybrid working model designed to keep you energized and balanced.

Our Early Careers Programmes

Choose the path that fits your current stage of study.

2 YEARS

The Graduate Scheme

A rotational 24-month programme designed to give you comprehensive exposure across our entire business operations.

  • 4 rotations (Sales, Underwriting, Ops, Strategy)
  • Permanent contract from day one
  • £32,000 starting salary + performance bonuses
  • Full sponsorship for professional qualifications

10 WEEKS

The Summer Internship

An immersive 10-week summer internship for penultimate-year university students looking to secure a grad role early.

  • Dedicated real-world summer project
  • Highly competitive pro-rata salary
  • Networking events with senior leadership
  • Fast-track to a graduate offer for top performers

The Application Journey

We've designed our process to be transparent, fast, and focused on discovering your true potential.

1

Submit Your Application

Send us your CV and a brief cover letter outlining why you want to join Stonebridge. No lengthy psychometric tests required at this stage.

2

Initial Video Interview

A relaxed 30-minute chat with our Early Careers team to get to know you, understand your ambitions, and assess cultural fit.

3

Assessment Centre

A half-day immersive experience at our HQ. You’ll participate in a group case study, a partner interview, and get a chance to meet our current grads.

4

The Offer

We move fast. Expect to hear back within 48 hours of your assessment centre with a final decision and formal offer.
