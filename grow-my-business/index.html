What is a Grow My Business loan?

The UK government is extending its support for SMEs with new Grow My Business initiatives, launching on 1st July 2024. Announced in the Spring 2024 Budget, the Grow My Business scheme is an extension of the Recovery Loan Scheme (RLS), ensuring continued financial support for businesses across the UK.

Business loans FAQsHelpful answers before you apply

How do I get a business loan?

Start with an eligibility check, then we compare suitable lenders and guide you through application, approval, and payout.

What is the difference between secured and unsecured loans?

Unsecured loans do not require asset-backed security, while secured loans can provide higher limits and longer terms.

How quickly can funds be released?

In many cases, funding can be released quickly once underwriting and documentation are complete.

Can I apply with less-than-perfect credit?

Yes. Eligibility depends on your wider business profile and lender criteria, not just one score.

Will applying affect my credit score?

Initial eligibility conversations are designed to understand your needs before any formal lender checks. If a credit search is required later, we will explain that before it happens.

Do you work with start-ups?

Yes, some lenders can support newer businesses depending on the finance type, trading history, security, projections, and director background.

Is there a limit to how much I can borrow?

Funding amounts depend on the product, lender appetite, business profile, affordability, and available security. Stonebridge can support requirements from smaller working capital needs through to larger facilities.

Are there any sectors you do not support?

Most mainstream UK business sectors can be considered. Some lenders may have restrictions, so we check your sector and funding purpose early in the process.

Still undecided?

You can start with a simple conversation. We will help you understand what may be possible before you commit to a full application.
