FAQs

Common questions about our partner finance process

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Become a Partner
Is there a cost to join as a partner?

No — joining the Stonebridge partner network is completely free. There are no set-up fees, no annual fees, and no ongoing charges. We make our money when your customers successfully access finance, so our incentives are fully aligned with yours.

What happens after I refer a customer?

Once you refer a customer, one of our business finance specialists will contact them within the hour. From there, we handle everything — the assessment, application, lender search, and documentation. You'll receive updates throughout the process so you always know what's happening.

How long does the whole process take?

In most cases, from initial referral to funds being released takes 24–72 hours. Some straightforward cases can be approved and funded on the same day. Larger or more complex finance requirements may take a little longer, but we'll always keep you and your customer informed at every stage.

Do I need to be involved throughout the process?

Not at all. Once you've made the referral, we take care of everything. You won't need to fill in any forms, chase paperwork, or manage the lender relationship. Our team handles the entire process on your behalf, letting you focus on running your business.

When and how do I get paid?

Payment is released to you upon receipt of the signed finance agreement from your customer. This means you get paid faster — often before you've even delivered the goods or services — with zero credit risk to your business.

What types of finance are available to my customers?

We offer access to a wide range of commercial finance products including asset finance, equipment finance, business loans, hire purchase, leasing and more — with terms from 3 months to 10 years and finance amounts from £1,000 to several million pounds.

What are the eligibility requirements for my customers?

Requirements vary by lender, but in general your customers should be a UK-registered business with at least 6 months of trading history. Our team will carry out a full assessment and will identify the most suitable lenders based on your customer's profile and circumstances.
